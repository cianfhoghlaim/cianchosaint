## 1. Wales-specific carve-outs

- **Defence**: UK-wide shared (Sennybridge + Castlemartin + Brecon are in Wales but the data is UK MoD-scoped).
- **Policing**: 4 forces (Dyfed-Powys + Gwent + North Wales + South Wales) — devolved but bilingual Welsh-medium (cy) coverage is required for the public-facing data.
- **Intel oversight**: shared IPCO + Welsh Information Commissioner.
- **Public inquiries**: devolved.
- **Emergency services**: Welsh Ambulance Services NHS Trust + 3 Welsh FRS.
- **Audit**: Wales Audit Office (WAO), NOT NAO.
