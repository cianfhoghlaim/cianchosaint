## 1. Scotland-specific carve-outs

- **Defence**: UK-wide shared (HMNB Clyde + Leuchars + Faslane are in Scotland but the data is UK MoD-scoped).
- **Policing**: devolved — Police Scotland single force since 2013 + the Scottish Police Authority (SPA) + HMICS.
- **Intel oversight**: shared IPCO + Scottish Information Commissioner.
- **Public inquiries**: devolved — Scottish COVID-19 Inquiry + Edinburgh Tram Inquiry.
- **Emergency services**: Scottish Ambulance Service + SFRS.
- **Audit**: Audit Scotland (NOT NAO — that's England/Wales/NI only).
