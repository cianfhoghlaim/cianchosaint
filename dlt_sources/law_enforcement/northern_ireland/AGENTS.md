## 1. NI-specific carve-outs

- **Defence**: UK-wide shared but the NI estate (Aldergrove + Ballykinler + Lisburn) is locally documented.
- **Policing**: PSNI single force + NI Policing Board oversight + DOJ(NI).
- **Intel oversight**: NI Policing Board + IPCO NI + NI Human Rights Commission.
- **Public inquiries**: devolved (UK COVID-19 Inquiry NI Module + HIA + Muckamore + Patrick Finucane).
- **Emergency services**: NIAS + NIFRS + NI Community Safety.
- **Audit**: NI Audit Office (NIAO).
